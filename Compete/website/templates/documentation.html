1. Introduction

What is Compete CTF?

Compete CTF is a comprehensive Capture The Flag (CTF) training platform designed to help cybersecurity enthusiasts, students, and professionals develop their skills through hands-on challenges. The platform offers three main categories of challenges: Cyber Security, Command-Line Interface (CLI), and Programming.

What are CTF Challenges?

CTF (Capture The Flag) challenges are security competitions where participants solve various tasks to find hidden "flags" (strings of text). Each challenge tests different skills and provides an opportunity to learn new techniques in a safe, controlled environment.

Key Features:
  • Three distinct challenge categories
  • Multiple difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard)
  • User profile and progress tracking
  • Community-driven challenge creation
  • Real-world skill development

2. Getting Started

Creating an Account

Follow these simple steps to get started with Compete CTF:

1 Navigate to the Sign Up page by clicking the button in the top-right corner of the homepage.

2 Fill in the registration form with the following information:

  • Username (your unique identifier on the platform)
  • Email address (for account recovery and notifications)
  • Password (choose a strong password)
  • Password confirmation

3 Click Sign Up to create your account.

4 You'll be automatically logged in and redirected to the homepage.

Logging In

If you already have an account, click the Login button in the navigation bar and enter your username and password. You can also use the "Remember Me" option to stay logged in.

Password Recovery

Forgot your password? Click the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page. You'll receive an email with instructions to reset your password securely.

Pro Tip: Update your profile information after signing up to personalize your experience and let other competitors know more about you!

3. Challenge Categories

Compete CTF offers three main categories of challenges, each designed to test different skill sets:

Cyber Security Challenges

Test your cybersecurity knowledge and skills with challenges covering offensive and defensive security topics.

Categories:

  • Red Team: Offensive security, penetration testing, exploitation techniques
  • Blue Team: Defensive security, incident response, security monitoring
  • Miscellaneous: General cybersecurity topics, forensics, cryptography, and more

Command-Line Interface (CLI) Challenges

Master command-line skills across different operating systems and shell environments.

Categories:

  • Linux CLI: Bash commands, shell scripting, Linux system administration
  • Windows CLI: CMD commands, batch scripting, Windows system management
  • PowerShell: PowerShell scripting, automation, and administration
  • Miscellaneous: Cross-platform CLI tools, custom shells, and specialized environments

Programming Challenges

Sharpen your programming and problem-solving skills with algorithmic and coding challenges.

Categories:

  • Python: Python programming, algorithms, data structures
  • JavaScript: JavaScript/Node.js programming, web development
  • C++: C++ programming, performance optimization, system programming
  • Java: Java programming, object-oriented design, enterprise patterns
  • Miscellaneous: Other programming languages and cross-language challenges

Difficulty Levels

Each challenge is tagged with a difficulty level to help you choose appropriate challenges:

Easy Beginner-friendly challenges requiring basic knowledge
Medium Intermediate challenges requiring solid understanding
Hard Advanced challenges requiring expert knowledge and creativity
Recommendation: Start with Easy challenges to familiarize yourself with the platform and challenge format, then gradually progress to more difficult challenges as you improve.

4. Submitting Flags

Accessing Challenges

To view challenges in any category:

  1. Click on the desired category in the navigation bar (Cyber, CLI, or Programming)
  2. Select View Challenges from the dropdown menu
  3. Browse the list of available challenges
  4. Click on any challenge to view its details

Understanding Challenge Details

Each challenge page displays:

  • Title: The challenge name
  • Difficulty: Easy, Medium, or Hard
  • Category: Specific subcategory within the main category
  • Description: Detailed instructions and background information
  • Flag Submission Form: Where you enter your answer

Finding and Submitting Flags

  1. Read the description carefully - All information needed to solve the challenge is provided
  2. Work through the challenge - Use the skills and tools relevant to the challenge category
  3. Identify the flag - Flags are typically strings in formats like:
    flag{example_flag_here}
    CTF{another_flag_format}
    COMPETE{challenge_solved}
  4. Enter the flag - Type or paste the complete flag into the submission form
  5. Submit - Click the submit button to verify your answer
Important:
  • Flags are case-sensitive unless otherwise specified
  • Include the entire flag format (e.g., "flag{}" brackets)
  • Whitespace matters - copy flags exactly as you find them
  • There are no attempt limits, so feel free to try different approaches

After Solving

Once you successfully submit a correct flag, you'll receive immediate feedback. Your progress is automatically tracked in your profile, allowing you to see which challenges you've completed.

6. Creating Challenges (Staff Only)

Note: Challenge creation is restricted to staff members only. If you're interested in contributing challenges, please contact an administrator.

For Staff Members

If you have staff privileges, you can create new challenges to expand the platform:

Steps to Create a Challenge:

  1. Navigate to the appropriate category (Cyber, CLI, or Programming)
  2. Click New Challenge from the dropdown menu
  3. Fill in the challenge creation form:
    • Title: A clear, descriptive name for the challenge
    • Description: Detailed instructions and context (supports formatted text)
    • Difficulty: Select Easy, Medium, or Hard
    • Category: Choose the appropriate subcategory
    • Flag: The correct answer/flag for the challenge
  4. Review your challenge for clarity and accuracy
  5. Click Create Challenge to publish

Challenge Creation Best Practices:

  • Clear Instructions: Provide all necessary information without being overly explicit
  • Appropriate Difficulty: Ensure the difficulty level matches the challenge complexity
  • Test Your Challenge: Verify that the challenge is solvable and the flag is correct
  • Educational Value: Ensure competitors will learn something valuable
  • Unique Content: Create original challenges that aren't easily found elsewhere

7. Tips & Best Practices

General Tips

Start Simple: Begin with Easy challenges to understand the format and build confidence
Read Carefully: Challenge descriptions often contain subtle hints or important details
Take Notes: Document your approach, findings, and solutions for future reference
Learn from Failures: Incorrect submissions are opportunities to learn and refine your approach
Use Resources: Leverage online documentation, tutorials, and tools relevant to each challenge

Category-Specific Tips

Cyber Security Challenges

  • Familiarize yourself with common security tools (Burp Suite, Wireshark, Metasploit, etc.)
  • Understand basic networking concepts and protocols
  • Learn about web vulnerabilities (OWASP Top 10)
  • Practice in a safe, isolated environment

CLI Challenges

  • Learn basic commands for your target operating system
  • Understand file system navigation and permissions
  • Practice text processing with tools like grep, sed, and awk
  • Use man pages and help commands (man, --help, Get-Help)

Programming Challenges

  • Understand fundamental algorithms and data structures
  • Practice reading and analyzing code
  • Test your solutions with different inputs
  • Consider edge cases and boundary conditions

Effective Learning Strategies

Progressive Learning Path:
  1. Master the basics in one category before moving to advanced challenges
  2. Diversify across categories to develop well-rounded skills
  3. Revisit challenging problems after learning new techniques
  4. Set personal goals and track your progress
  5. Challenge yourself regularly with harder difficulty levels

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Account & Access

Q: Is Compete CTF free to use?

A: Yes, creating an account and participating in challenges is completely free.

Q: Can I delete my account?

A: Please contact an administrator if you wish to delete your account.

Q: I forgot my password. What should I do?

A: Use the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page to reset your password via email.

Challenges

Q: Are there limits on how many times I can attempt a challenge?

A: No, you can attempt challenges as many times as needed. There are no penalties for incorrect submissions.

Q: What if I can't solve a challenge?

A: Take a break and try a different challenge. Sometimes fresh perspective helps. You can also research the relevant topics and return to the challenge later.

Q: Can I see solutions after solving a challenge?

A: The platform currently doesn't provide official solutions, but you can document your own approach for future reference.

Q: How often are new challenges added?

A: New challenges are added regularly by staff members. Check back frequently for new content!

Technical Issues

Q: The website isn't working properly. What should I do?

A: Try the following steps:

  • Refresh the page (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R)
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies
  • Try a different browser
  • Check your internet connection
  • If issues persist, contact support

Q: I submitted the correct flag but it's showing as incorrect. Why?

A: Double-check the following:

  • Flag format is correct (including brackets, underscores, etc.)
  • No extra spaces before or after the flag
  • Correct capitalization (flags are case-sensitive)
  • You're submitting the complete flag

Contributing

Q: Can I create and submit my own challenges?

A: Challenge creation is currently limited to staff members. If you're interested in contributing, please reach out to the administrators.

Q: I found a bug or have a feature suggestion. How can I report it?

A: Please contact the platform administrators with details about the bug or your feature request.

Privacy & Security

Q: Is my personal information secure?

A: Yes, we take security seriously. Your password is encrypted, and we follow best practices for data protection.

Q: What information is visible to other users?

A: Your username and any information you add to your profile (name, website, about section) may be visible to other users. Your email address and password are always private.

Need More Help?

If you have questions that aren't answered in this documentation, please reach out to the platform administrators or staff members for assistance.
