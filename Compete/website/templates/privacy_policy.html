Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 30, 2024

1. Introduction

Compete CTF ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Capture The Flag platform. Please read this policy carefully.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you register for an account, we collect:

  • Username: Your chosen display name for the platform
  • Email Address: Used for account verification, password resets, and important platform notifications
  • Password: Securely hashed and stored using industry-standard encryption
  • Profile Information: Optional information such as first name, last name, about section, and website URL

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use the platform, we automatically collect:

  • Challenge Activity: Records of challenges you attempt and solve, including timestamps and earned points
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the platform, including pages visited and features used
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information, and session data for security and platform functionality

2.3 Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to maintain your session, remember your preferences, and improve platform functionality. These are essential for the platform to work correctly.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: To create, maintain, and secure your user account
  • Platform Functionality: To provide access to challenges, track your progress, and display leaderboards
  • Authentication: To verify your identity and manage login sessions
  • Communication: To send password reset emails and important platform updates
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and respond to security incidents, abuse, and fraudulent activity
  • Performance Monitoring: To analyze platform usage and improve our services
  • Leaderboards: To display your username, points, and ranking in competition with other users

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 Public Information

The following information is publicly visible on the platform:

  • Your username
  • Your leaderboard position and total points
  • Challenge completion statistics (if you choose to make your profile public)

4.2 Information We Don't Share

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We will not share your information except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Compete CTF, our users, or the public
  • With your explicit consent

5. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your information:

  • Password Security: Passwords are hashed using Django's built-in secure password hashing (PBKDF2 with SHA256)
  • HTTPS Encryption: All data transmission is encrypted using SSL/TLS in production environments
  • CSRF Protection: Cross-Site Request Forgery protection is enabled on all forms
  • SQL Injection Protection: We use Django's ORM to prevent SQL injection attacks
  • Access Controls: Strict authentication and authorization controls limit access to user data

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Update

You can access and update your profile information at any time through your account settings. You have the right to review and modify your personal information.

6.2 Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account at any time by contacting us. Upon account deletion, your personal information will be removed from our active databases, though some information may be retained in backups for a limited period.

6.3 Data Portability

You have the right to request a copy of your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your information within a reasonable timeframe, except where we are required to retain it for legal obligations.

8. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Third-Party Services

Our platform uses the following third-party services:

  • Semantic UI (CDN): For user interface styling
  • jQuery (CDN): For interactive functionality

These services may collect information as described in their respective privacy policies. We do not control third-party tracking technologies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

11. International Users

If you are accessing the platform from outside the country where our servers are located, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in a location where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through our platform or visit our Documentation page.

Your Consent

By using Compete CTF, you consent to the collection, use, and processing of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our platform.

Questions About Your Data?

We take your privacy seriously. If you have any questions about how we handle your data or wish to exercise your rights, please don't hesitate to reach out to us. See also our Terms of Service for more information about platform usage.
